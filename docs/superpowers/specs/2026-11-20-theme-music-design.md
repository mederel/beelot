# Design: a background music per table theme (US-164)

## Goal
An optional music that matches the table theme, off until the player turns it on.

## Decisions
- **Tracks**: seven loops of 77 to 94 s, one per theme, named like the theme, in `static/music/<theme>.ogg` (Vorbis, quality 1,
  under 1 MB each). Style: modern lounge / downtempo, in the spirit of chill-out compilations. They are generated by
  `tools/music/compose.py` (numpy and scipy): a warm pad, a deep sub bass, a soft groove (kick, shaker, hand drum, rim),
  a plucked arpeggio and a sparse lead (oud, flute, felt piano) that plays a phrase and answers it. Each theme has its own
  key, mode, tempo and feel (comic: D dorian with flute, classic-green: jazzy felt piano, midnight: dark oriental oud,
  sunset: bossa lounge, forest: airy kalimba, ruby: oud and darbuka, gilded: big pad and a steady kick). Earlier
  versions (cartoon ragtime, then a softer one) were found too electronic, then too muddy. The reverb tail is folded back and the
  final filters run on the loop repeated three times, so the loop has no click.
- **Settings**: `beelot.music-enabled` ("true" only; off by default) and `beelot.music-volume` (low 0.12, medium 0.25, high 0.45).
  Music is not tied to the sound effects switch, which is on by default.
- **Playback**: `music.js` owns one `Audio` with `loop = true`, `preload = "none"`; it is created at the first time music is
  wanted. `syncMusic()` is called after the settings, the theme and the view change and when the page's visibility changes.
  Wanted = switch on, view is `table`, page visible. A change of track or of wanted state fades over 400 ms.
- **Autoplay**: `play()` refused by the browser is remembered; the next `pointerdown` or `keydown` tries again. Nothing is shown.
- **Offline**: the service worker does not cache `/music/` (ranges, size); without the network the music is silent.
- **Server**: `ServeDir` already serves `.ogg` as `audio/ogg` with range requests; the CSP (`default-src 'self'`) allows it.

## Risks
Musical taste cannot be checked by tests; the tracks are replaceable files with the same names. Size of the package grows by ~5 MB.
