Bulgarian Belot

Bulgarian Belot (“bridge-belote”, US-215) is the third variant next to Classic Belote and Contrée, and Bulgarian (bg) is the fourth language of the interface, next to English, French and Dutch. The design is in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-bulgarian-belot-design.md.

The rules

  • Eight cards are dealt to each player (3, 2, 3); there is no upturned card.
  • Each player passes or bids a game. The games rank clubs < diamonds < hearts < spades < no trumps < all trumps and each bid must outrank the last. Three passes after a bid close the auction; four passes in a row redeal. There is no contra or re-contra.
  • The card order and points are those of the game bid (the engine's tables for a suit, no trumps and all trumps; every round is worth 162 points).
  • The declaring team must score more than its opponents. If it does not, it is inside («вътре»): its opponents score every point of the round, announcements included.
  • The match is played to the usual targets (1,000 points by default).

The sources named in the story could not be fetched when this was written, so these rules rest on belot.bg's and general Belote sources and on local practice: they must be checked with a Bulgarian player before release. Not delivered yet: the two-player version (it needs a two-seat table and a stock), contra and re-contra, the traditional 151-point target, and a Bulgarian privacy policy and invitation page (they stay in English).

In the code

Variant::Bulgarian (BULGARIAN in JSON and in the database) reuses the Contrée auction with ordinal bids: a BID call's value is the game's rank, 1 to 6 (bidding::bulgarian_rank). Scoring is Classic's. The bots bid the best game their hand estimate supports and play like in the other variants. A recorded deal is in fixtures/deals-bulgarian.jsonl and is replayed by crates/beelot-game/tests/bulgarian.rs.

The Bulgarian interface

web/src/lib/i18n/bg.ts translates every text of fr.ts and nl.ts; the language is chosen in Settings (Български). The worst-case layout checks (e2e/extremes.spec.ts) run in Bulgarian too, because Cyrillic is wider than Latin.
