Bela

Bela (also Belot) is the Croatian, Bosnian and Serbian cousin of Belote: four players, two partnerships, the 32 cards. Pick Bela in the setup sheet, the public lobby or the leaderboard. It is not Klaberjass (US-219), which is sometimes called Bela as well. The design and its open questions are in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-bela-design.md.

The rules Beelot plays

  • Deal: 3 then 3 cards (six each), then 2 more each once trumps are named. No card is turned up.
  • Auction: one round from the player after the dealer. Each player names the trump suit or says dalje (pass); the first to name a suit calls it. The dealer may not pass.
  • Play and cards: as in Classic Belote (follow suit, overtrump, trump when void unless the partner is winning); trump jack 20, nine 14, ace 11, ten 10, king 4, queen 3; 162 points with the last trick.
  • Zvanje: sequences of three (20), four (50), five or more (100); four jacks 200, four nines 150, four aces, tens, kings or queens 100; the bela (trump king and queen) 20. Always on, no switch.
  • Scoring: the calling team must score more than its opponents, or it is pad: the opponents get all 162 points and every declaration. All eight tricks (štiglja) score 252.
  • Match: to 1,000 points by default (500, 1,000, 1,500 or 2,000 can be chosen).

Not played yet

Two- and three-player Bela (no authoritative rule set was found), trumping over a partner that is winning (a point to confirm with a Croatian rulebook), and a bot threshold fitted in the arena (cargo run -p beelot-arena -- --variant bela plays Bela; both stays the two Belote variants).

Languages

The page is available in Croatian (Settings, Language). The Latin script also serves Bosnian and Serbian players; a Serbian Cyrillic locale and separate bs and sr locales are future work. A text without a Croatian translation shows in English.
