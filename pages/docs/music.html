Table music

Each table theme has its own loop in crates/beelot-server/web/public/music/<theme>.ogg (copied to static/music/ by the build). Players turn it on in Settings (Music, off by default) and choose its volume; it plays only at a table. The tracks are composed by tools/music/compose.py as MIDI (chords, bass, groove, a melody from a motif) and played by sampled instruments: it needs python3 with numpy, fluidsynth, ffmpeg with libvorbis and the GeneralUser GS soundfont (free for any use, commercial music included; the script looks for it in the usual places or in SOUNDFONT). python3 tools/music/compose.py out/ comic ruby writes the .mid, .wav and .ogg of each theme; the OGG files are the ones in web/public/music/. The result is the same each time.

Beelot is free software, AGPL-3.0-or-later. These pages are served by GitHub Pages next to the apt and dnf repositories.
