Bazar Blot

Bazar Blot («Բազար բլոտ», US-216) is the Belote of Armenia, played by four in two teams. Armenian (hy) is a language of the interface with it. The design, with the sources of every rule, is in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-bazar-blot-design.md.

The rules

  • Eight cards are dealt to each player (3, 2, 3); there is no upturned card.
  • The auction (the bazar) starts left of the dealer. Each player passes or bids a number of tens, 8 to 24, in a suit or in no trumps («boy»), or kaput (25), the bid of every trick. Each bid needs a higher number; three passes after a bid end the bazar, four passes in a row redeal.
  • An opponent of the highest bidder may say contra, which stops the bidding: the bidding team may then answer recontra, or pass.
  • The play follows Belote's rules. Declarations (terz 20, four in sequence 50, five 100, four of a kind 100 to 200, with four nines and four jacks higher in a suit) score for the team holding the best one; the king and queen of trumps (blot) always score 20.
  • The bidding team must take at least ten times its bid, counting its card points, last trick, declarations and blot (a kaput counts 252). Scores are kept in tens, a half rounding down: the deal is worth 16, or 25 for a kaput. The bid counts once in a suit and twice in no trumps, twice and four times under contra and recontra in a suit, three and five times in no trumps.
  • A made bid scores the bid and the deal for the bidders, less the defence's tens unless contra was said; the defence keeps its own tens. A failed bid gives the bid, the deal and every declaration to the opponents; the bidders keep their blot.
  • The match is played to 301 by default, or to 101, 151 or 201.

Armenian sources disagree on details, and the ones named in the story could not be fetched; these rules follow reblot.am, bazarblot.am and belote.club, and must be checked with an Armenian player before release. Not delivered yet: the Armenian privacy policy and invitation page (they stay in English), and the two- and three-player games.

In the code

Variant::BazarBlot (BAZAR_BLOT in JSON and in the database) reuses the Contrée auction with bids in tens; contra is the coinche call and CallType::Recontra answers it. board::BazarContract holds the contract and scoring::score_bazar_blot the score in tens (scoring::sai). The bots bid their Contrée estimate divided by ten, never kaput, say contra when they expect to defeat the bid and recontra when their estimate is well above it. Recorded deals are in fixtures/deals-bazar-blot.jsonl and are replayed by crates/beelot-game/tests/bazar_blot.rs; the arena plays it with --variant bazar-blot.

The Armenian interface

web/src/lib/i18n/hy.ts translates every text in Eastern Armenian; the language is chosen in Settings (Հայերեն). Geist and Bricolage have no Armenian letters, so both font stacks fall back to Noto Sans Armenian (SIL OFL, @fontsource-variable/noto-sans-armenian), served by the site and downloaded only for Armenian characters (its unicode-range). tests/i18n/armenian.test.ts fails when a character of hy.ts is in no declared font range. The worst-case layout checks run in Armenian, whose words are the longest of the languages, and the home screen is photographed in Armenian.
