Beelot is a card game table for Belote and Contrée.

Play Classic Belote or Contrée (with bids, coinche and surcoinche) at a table of four. Sit down against bots that play, comment and react like real opponents, or invite friends to a private table.

What you get:
• Classic Belote and Contrée, with announcements (tierce, cinquante, cent, carré) and Belote–Rebelote
• Bots of several levels, with a tutorial and the rules one tap away
• Hints and a review of your rounds, to learn from your plays
• Private tables with a code, and public matchmaking
• Ratings and a leaderboard, if you want to compete
• English, French and Dutch

Play as a guest, with nothing to sign up for. If you want to keep your history, create an account with a passkey: no email address and no password. You can download your data or delete your account in the settings at any time.

Beelot has no ads and no trackers. Its privacy policy is at beelot.fr/privacy.
